Privacy Policy — Map Puzzle: Countries & States

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Map Puzzle: Countries & States (package com.mappuzzle.world) handles information. The app is made by abiGence Solutions ("we").

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your progress, stars, hints and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, an occasional full-screen ad between maps, and a video ad only when you choose to watch one for a free hint. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app interaction and diagnostic data in order to show ads, limit ad frequency, measure performance and prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security and retention

Data shared with Google is encrypted in transit. Because we keep no data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete; data held by Google is governed by Google's policies.
